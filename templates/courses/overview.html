COURSE OFFERINGS

Publish a course

An offering connects a catalogue course to a programme, academic semester and student level. Lecturer assignments and student registration are managed against the offering.

Create offeringView offerings
Bulk import: Upload a validated CSV containing course catalogue records and programme-specific offerings. The importer previews all changes, preserves existing records and keeps registration closed until an administrator opens it.
Data model: A course is the reusable catalogue record. A course offering is the delivery of that course to a particular programme and level in a semester. A lecturer is assigned to an offering; students register for the offering.
