# RollNexa Brand Guidelines — Version 1.0

## Brand name
**RollNexa**

## Tagline
**Attendance, connected.**

## Product category
Smart Academic Attendance Platform.

## Brand positioning
RollNexa is a web-based academic attendance platform designed to connect student identity, attendance-session access and institutional attendance records in one coherent workflow. The product roadmap includes QR-code attendance and facial identity verification, supported by role-based access for administrators, lecturers and students.

## Brand idea
The name combines *roll call* with *nexus*: an academic roll call connected to people, sessions, courses and records.

## Brand attributes
- Trustworthy and accountable
- Precise and organised
- Connected and intelligent
- Accessible and approachable
- Academic without appearing bureaucratic

## Logo concept
The symbol represents a digital attendance register. Three student/record indicators sit within a document form, while a connected verification badge signals a confirmed record. The horizontal logo is for light backgrounds; the standalone mark is for favicons and compact navigation.

### Clear space and minimum size
- Maintain clear space around the mark equal to at least the width of one small indicator dot.
- Do not stretch, rotate, add shadows, or recolour the logo outside the approved palette.
- Use the horizontal logo at 150 px wide or larger where possible.
- Use the standalone mark for compact placements.

## Typography
- Display and headings: Manrope, with system sans-serif fallback.
- Body and interface: Inter, with system sans-serif fallback.
- Use sentence case for interface labels.
- Keep paragraphs concise and navigation labels explicit.

## Voice and writing
- Direct, calm and precise.
- Prefer specific actions such as “Create attendance session” and “Verify attendance”.
- Do not claim facial recognition is infallible or that a QR code alone proves a student's identity.
- Clearly distinguish implemented features from planned features.
- Explain errors without exposing stack traces, server paths, secrets or configuration.

## Product status and claims
RollNexa 0.2.1 is a software prototype with user management and role-based dashboards. QR attendance, facial enrollment/verification, course management and attendance reporting remain planned modules unless subsequently implemented and tested. Do not imply institutional endorsement, regulatory certification or production-grade biometric assurance.
